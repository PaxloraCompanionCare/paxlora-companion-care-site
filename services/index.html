Support thatfits into real life.

Thoughtful, dependable companion care centered around the person—not just the task.

Paxlora Companion Care provides non-medical support designed around each individual's routines, preferences, personality and needs. Our goal is to provide consistency, meaningful connection and peace of mind for the people we serve and the families who love them.

Our Services

Companionship

Meaningful conversation, social engagement and genuine human connection. This can include spending time together at home, enjoying hobbies and activities, playing games, reading, taking walks, or simply being present.

Meal Preparation

Thoughtful meal preparation based on your loved one's preferences and established routines—from preparing simple meals and snacks to helping keep mealtime comfortable and familiar.

Light Housekeeping

Everyday household support that helps maintain a comfortable and welcoming environment, including light tidying, dishes, laundry and keeping commonly used areas organized.

Wellness & Routine Reminders

Gentle reminders and encouragement that help your loved one stay connected to established daily routines, appointments, meals, hydration and other everyday wellness habits.

Consistent Support

Dependable companion care focused on familiarity and trust. Whenever possible, we prioritize consistency so your loved one isn't constantly adjusting to unfamiliar faces.

Is Paxlora rightfor your loved one?

Paxlora may be a good fit for older adults, adults with disabilities, individuals with special needs, and others who benefit from dependable companionship and everyday non-medical support.
