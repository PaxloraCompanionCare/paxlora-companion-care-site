Paxlora Companion Care ("Paxlora," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information you share with us through paxloracompanioncare.com (the "Site") and when you contact us about our non-medical companion care services.

Information We Collect

When you submit our consultation request form, call, or email us, we may collect information such as your name, phone number, email address, information about the person you are seeking care for, and any details you choose to share about the type or amount of support you are looking for.

We do not ask you to submit diagnoses, medication lists, Social Security numbers, insurance information, or other sensitive medical information through our website forms, and we ask that you avoid including this type of information when contacting us online.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule a consultation
  • To determine whether Paxlora Companion Care may be a good fit for your family's needs
  • To communicate with you about our services
  • To maintain records related to our business operations

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate our business (such as our website hosting or form-processing provider), or as required by law.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may contact us at any time at Info@paxloracompanioncare.com to ask questions about the information we hold about you or to request that we remove information you've shared with us that is not otherwise required for our business or legal records.

Children's Privacy

Our Site is intended for adults seeking companion care services on behalf of themselves or a loved one and is not directed at children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page.
